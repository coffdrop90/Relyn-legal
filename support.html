How do I add a commitment?

Tap the + button on any screen and either type it out or speak it using Smart Capture — say something like "call Jason tomorrow about pricing" and Relyn will pull out the category, priority, due date, and contact automatically. You can also say "Hey Siri, add a commitment to Relyn" without opening the app at all.

Does Relyn sync across my devices?

Yes, automatically. Your commitments mirror across every device signed into the same Apple ID — including the Apple Watch app — through your own iCloud account, with nothing to turn on or configure. Relyn's makers never see or store that data.

I'm not getting reminder notifications.

Check Settings → Notifications → Relyn on your iPhone and make sure Allow Notifications is turned on. If you dismissed the permission prompt on first launch, this is the only way to re-enable it — Relyn can't re-ask for you.

How do recurring commitments work?

When adding or editing a commitment, set a Repeat interval (Daily, Weekly, Every 2 Weeks, or Monthly). Completing it automatically creates the next occurrence with the due date moved forward — you never have to re-enter a recurring task by hand.

What does the Home Screen widget show?

Your next due commitment along with how many are active and overdue. It updates automatically whenever you add, complete, or change something in the app — no manual refresh needed.

Is my data private?

Yes — Relyn collects nothing and makes no network calls of its own. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
